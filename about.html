OUR STORY

///

A football community in Agra, open to anyone who loves the game.

Why This Exists

Agra didn't have a real football community, not the way running clubs and cycling groups already did. We didn't set out to build something formal. We started The Offside Club because we love this game, and we wanted a way for anyone who feels the same to actually play it, watch it, and be around people who care about it just as much.

That's really the whole idea. Not a grassroots project, not an academy. Just a football community, open to everyone, built entirely around the game itself. No trials to get in, no minimum skill level, no politics about who gets to play.

If you love football, there's a place for you here.

WHAT WE DO

///
01

Regular Games

Regular games are the core of it: matches running twice a week, moving across different turfs in Agra so it's never stuck to one corner of the city. Different ground, different crowd, same energy. No tryouts, no bench. If you show up wanting to play, you're playing, whether it's your first match ever or your five-hundredth.

02

Tournaments

Beyond the weekly games, we run regular tournaments to keep things sharp and competitive. The kind of games where bragging rights actually matter, where a group chat argument finally gets settled on the pitch instead of in text messages.

03

Watch Together

Football isn't just something we play, it's something we watch together too. We get together for screenings of the matches that matter: Premier League Saturdays, La Liga nights, Champions League evenings that go long past when they should. Big screen, loud room, a community that shows up for the football on screen as much as the football on the turf.

04

The In-Between

And in between all of that, there's room for the smaller stuff, the kind of games that don't need an occasion. Crossbar challenges. 1v1s that turn into arguments about who actually won. The stuff that takes a random Tuesday evening and makes it worth showing up for, even if there's no real match happening.

THE PEOPLE

///

None of this works without the people in it. This isn't a club with members and a membership card. It's a group of people who kept showing up, brought their friends, and made the whole thing bigger than any single game could be.

The football brought us together. Everything else: the friendships, the running jokes, the group chat that never sleeps. That's what actually kept us coming back.

Est. 2026 · Agra

Founded in 2026, in Agra. Started with a handful of people who just wanted somewhere to play, and it's grown every single week since. Not because of anything we did especially right, but because it turns out a lot of people in this city were waiting for exactly this.

If you're one of them, this is where you start.
